Decentralized clinical trials (DCTs) have revolutionized the way clinical research is conducted. By utilizing technology to reach participants remotely, these trials have opened doors to faster enrollment, greater diversity, and enhanced convenience for both researchers and participants. However, as with any technology-driven process, data security in decentralized clinical trials has become a critical concern.
