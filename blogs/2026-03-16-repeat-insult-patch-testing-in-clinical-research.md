For brands developing topical products like skincare, cosmetics, or personal care items, understanding how a formulation interacts with human skin is essential. Consumers expect products to be safe and non-irritating, and regulators expect brands to substantiate those expectations. One of the most widely used tools for evaluating skin compatibility is Repeat Insult Patch Testing (RIPT).
